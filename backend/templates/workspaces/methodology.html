{# Area targets — mandated by the transparency principle: no score or projection may be a black box, so every factor is printed with its source. #}

{% trans "Area targets and impact projection" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A target area combines a polygon with a goal. The baseline is counted from this workspace's own accident records inside that polygon; the projection applies published effect factors to the segments proposed for rebuilding. It is a planning estimate with a stated range, not a forecast of individual events, and regression to the mean is not corrected for. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Targets for people killed or seriously injured are always zero." %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} A target such as “90 % fewer road deaths” declares the remaining deaths acceptable. OpenMobility OS does not offer percentage targets for those indicators and cannot store one — and where a plan does not reach zero, it reports the number of people still expected to be harmed rather than a percentage that hides them. {% endblocktrans %}
